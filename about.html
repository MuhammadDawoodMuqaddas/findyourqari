How It Started

Our Story

Finding a trustworthy Quran teacher online can often be challenging. Many students and parents struggle to determine whether a teacher is genuinely qualified, experienced, and suitable for their learning needs. While numerous online tutoring platforms exist, many provide limited information about a teacher's qualifications, teaching style, or experience — making it difficult to make an informed decision.

FindYourQari was created to solve this challenge.

Our platform was built with a simple vision: to make it easier for students worldwide to connect with qualified Quran teachers through a transparent and trusted platform. Instead of focusing on becoming the largest directory of teachers, we focus on maintaining high standards by carefully reviewing every teacher who joins our community.

We believe that learning the Quran is not just about completing lessons — it's about building confidence, strengthening faith, and creating a lifelong connection with the words of Allah.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make authentic Quran education accessible to every student by connecting learners with qualified, verified, and compassionate Quran teachers through personalized online learning.

We are committed to providing an educational experience that combines traditional Islamic knowledge with the convenience of modern technology, allowing students to learn from anywhere in the world.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the world's most trusted online Quran learning platforms by promoting excellence in teaching, transparency in teacher verification, and meaningful learning experiences for students and families worldwide.

We aspire to create a global community where quality Quran education is available to everyone, regardless of their location or background.
